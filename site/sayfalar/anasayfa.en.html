__KANCA_KARTLARI__

Why does it work?

Memorized words fade because they hold on to nothing. A hook gives each word something to hold on to.

A link beats memorizing

Your brain lets go of isolated facts and keeps what’s attached to something it already knows. snake hooks onto sinek (fly), a word you’ve known for years — the new word is built on an old one.

Sound and scene: two traces

The word lives both in your ear (sinek) and in your mind’s eye (a snake swallowing a fly). If one fades, the other brings it back.

Recalling beats rereading

Rereading a word feels familiar but doesn’t make it stick. When the card is hidden and you bring the word back yourself, the trace gets deeper — that’s why the six steps climb from choosing to typing to listening.
